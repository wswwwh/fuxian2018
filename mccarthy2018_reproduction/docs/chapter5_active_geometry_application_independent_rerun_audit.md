scan, a 1-degree local refinement, and a 0.25-degree tight target scan around
the best phase. The coarse and 1-degree scans are retained as failure/boundary
evidence; the tight scan passes the application gate.

- Best phase: `(theta0, theta1) = (48.0, 62.0)` degrees
